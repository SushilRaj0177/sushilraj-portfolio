Shiftseal: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Shiftseal keeps a tamper-evident record of working time, pay, leave and safety reports for the people who work at a workplace and for the employer who runs it. It is made by an independent developer (Sushil Raj, sushilraj0177@gmail.com). This policy covers the Shiftseal Android app and the web app, and says what is collected, why, who else handles it, how long it is kept and how to delete it.

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics trackers, and nothing is ever sold or rented.
  • Your location is read only at the moment you check in or out, never in the background.
  • You can delete your account any time in Settings, and your name and number are erased straight away.

What is collected, and why

Voice notes and AI (Google Gemini)

When you hold the talk button, or type a hazard report, an overtime claim, a question about your payslip or anonymous feedback, the text or recording is sent to Google's Gemini model on Vertex AI so it can be understood. Voice recordings are never stored: they are turned into text and discarded. Google does not use this data to train its models.

The AI only reads and structures what you say. Every number you see (hours, pay, deadlines) is calculated by Shiftseal's own rules, not by the AI.

Anonymous feedback

Names and places are removed before anything is saved. Your words are not kept, only the cleaned summary, and your employer sees feedback only once at least five people in your team have spoken.

Who else handles it

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run and Firestore) stores and serves the records.
  • Firebase Authentication handles sign-in.
  • Gemini on Vertex AI reads the text and voice notes you choose to send, as described above.
  • Google Play Integrity checks that the Android app is genuine.

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. These are service providers working for Shiftseal; your data is not shared with anyone else.

Permissions

  • Location: read once, at the moment you check in or out.
  • Camera: to scan the gate QR code and to photograph a hazard.
  • Microphone: only while you hold the talk button.
  • Internet / network state: to send your checks and to keep them queued while you are offline.

How long it is kept

Work records are kept while your workplace uses Shiftseal, because labour law requires employers to keep time and wage records (for example, three years in India and the United States). Your sign-in details and profile are kept until you delete your account.

Children

Shiftseal is for working people and their employers. It is not directed at children under 13. Where the law allows young people to work (for example, 14 to 17 in some countries), Shiftseal applies the extra hour and night-work limits for them.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete my account. Your name, phone number, email, student status, date of birth and device keys are erased straight away, and you leave every workplace. Shift records that were already sealed stay with your employer under "Former member", without your name or number, because the law requires wage records to be kept and the sealed chain cannot be edited. If you cannot open the app, or you own a workplace that still has people in it, email sushilraj0177@gmail.com from any address with the phone number or email you signed in with, and it will be done within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date, and the app will show it.
