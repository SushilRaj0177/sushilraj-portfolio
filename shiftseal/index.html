The problem

Whoever holds the register controls the truth.

Overtime disappears

A checkout at 20:40 quietly becomes 18:00, and the worker has no way to prove it.

Workers stay silent

Complaints are rare, because people fear being identified.

Danger surfaces too late

Hazards are noticed only after someone gets hurt.

The principle

The AI understands. Code decides. Nothing reaches a worker unchecked.

Gemini understands workers who speak Tamil, Hindi or English, even casually, even by voice. Every number and decision that matters (hours, overtime, pay, danger) comes from tested rules.

01

A record that can’t be quietly changed

Every check-in and check-out is chained with SHA-256, and the worker’s phone keeps its own copy. A supervisor can correct a mistake, but only with a written reason, and the worker is told within seconds, in his language.

Hash-chained ledgerMandatory reasonsTamil · Hindi · English

02

AI that is checked before it speaks

When a worker asks why his pay is lower, Gemini explains in plain language, but every number is checked against his real payslip first. If one is wrong, the answer is thrown away.

Fact-checked answersSchema-constrained output

03

Overtime claims backed by evidence

“I stayed two extra hours on Saturday,” said in Tamil. The AI understands it, the sealed shift records prove it, and the employer must settle it before payday.

Voice in any languageMatched to receipts

04

A safety rule the AI can’t override

Workers report danger by zone. A fall from height is never minor: the AI can raise the severity, never lower it. Feedback stays truly anonymous: identities are removed before saving and shown only once five people speak.
