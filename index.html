About

I’m a CS undergraduate at SRMIST in Chennai, specialising in Software Engineering. I’m interested in what goes into building good software — the architecture, the decisions behind it, and the details that make it reliable.

So far, I’ve worked on web applications, real-time systems, and tools designed to solve practical problems. I enjoy working across the stack, trying out unfamiliar technologies, and figuring out how things work beyond just getting them to run.

Outside of software, I’m learning Japanese through the JLPT track and currently preparing for N3, while working towards an opportunity to study at Waseda University in Tokyo. Japan and Japanese aesthetics are also a big influence on my projects, shaping the ideas I explore and the way I approach design.

I’m focused on building better software, strengthening my engineering skills, and taking on projects that push me to learn something new.
